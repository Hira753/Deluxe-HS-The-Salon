About Deluxe HS The Salon

Discover the story behind Faisalabad's most cherished executive beauty & bridal studio.

Founded With Passion

Redefining Salon Standards with Artistry & Hygiene

Deluxe HS The Salon was created by Master Stylist Hira Shah with a clear vision: to establish a sanctuary of luxury where every woman experiences individualized, high-end beauty care without compromising on health or hygiene.

Located in Block A of People's Colony No 1 (near Faysal Hospital), Faisalabad, our studio features state-of-the-art hair restoration stations, private bridal preparation suites, and organic spa therapy rooms.

100% Certified Organic Formulations Formaldehyde-free hair botox, ammonia-free colors, & dermatological facials.
